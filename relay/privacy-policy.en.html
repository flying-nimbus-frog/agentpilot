AgentPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who We Are

AgentPilot is an app that lets you control the AI assistants on your computer from your phone. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. By using the app, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. What We Collect

  • Account information: Your email address and password at registration. Passwords are stored only as encrypted hashes; we cannot read them.
  • Device information: Names of computers you pair and the pairing relationships, used to connect your phone to your computers.
  • Push identifiers: Device tokens for delivering notifications such as permission requests (provided by Apple/Google push services).

3. What We Do NOT Collect (Important)

  • We do not store your conversation content: Your chats, code, and files stay on your own computer locally.
  • We do not read your code or projects: The relay server only forwards commands and status events; it never parses or stores business content.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or other sensitive data: The app does not request any such permissions.
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs: We do not track your behavior.

4. How We Use Information

  • Provide core features: account login, device pairing, command forwarding
  • Send notifications you trigger (permission requests, task completion)
  • Protect service security (login rate limiting, anomaly detection)

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only when required by law, to protect service security, or with your explicit consent.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Account and device information is stored on our servers with end-to-end TLS encryption. We use industry-standard password hashing and rate limiting to protect your data. Data is retained to provide the service until you delete your account; after deletion, all associated data is permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

7. Your Rights

  • Delete your account: Use "More → Delete Account" in the app to permanently delete your account and all associated data (device bindings, push settings). This action cannot be undone.
  • Unbind devices at any time within the app.
  • Stop notifications by disabling the notification permission in system settings.
  • Access or correct your account information by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. If we become aware of a child's account, we will delete the account and associated data as required by law. Parents or guardians may contact us using the details below.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
